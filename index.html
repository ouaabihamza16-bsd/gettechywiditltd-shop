About us

Hello, we're Get Techy Wid It

GET TECHY WID IT LTD is an online retailer of tech products. We're a small, independent business based in Chessington, Surrey, and we run entirely online, so there's no shop counter or showroom to visit.

The company was incorporated on 9 December 2025 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 16900297. It's a fresh start, and we want to keep things straightforward from day one.

Our idea is simple: tech shouldn't feel complicated. We'd rather talk about it in plain English than hide behind jargon.
